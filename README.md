# Nalam – Family Check-In App

A gentle, one-tap check-in app that gives families peace of mind about elderly parents living alone. *Nalam* means "well-being" in Tamil.

🔗 **Live demo:** https://nalam-family-check-in-app--aishuniru203.replit.app

## The Problem
Many elders live far from their children, and calling several times a day can feel intrusive. Families want to know their loved ones are okay without constant calls.

## How It Works

**Elder view**
- Big, easy-to-tap buttons: **I'm fine 👍**, **I took my medicine 💊** and **I need help 🚨**
- The help button works by press-and-hold for 3 seconds or tap-to-confirm, which prevents accidental alerts
- Friendly confirmation messages like "Your family has been notified"

**Family view**
- Today's status at a glance, with expected and actual check-in times
- Live updates every 5 seconds
- Today's timeline of check-ins, medicine taken and help requests
- Medicine status and recent days' history
- Quick **Call** and **Message** buttons when help is requested

## Features
- One-tap check-ins designed for elders
- Real-time status for family members
- Emergency help alert with accidental-press protection
- Medicine tracking
- Activity timeline and recent history
- Bilingual interface (English and Tamil)
- Mobile-friendly dark UI
